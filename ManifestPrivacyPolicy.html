Privacy Policy & Terms

Manifest AI asks you personal questions that are used to create manifestations and to remember your preferences. Your responses are not sent to any other party for any reason.

An exception is when you interact with the AI to generate manifestations. Your responses are sent to the AI provider, so keep that in mind when conversing with the AI. Manifest AI does not record your conversation with the AI, it only records the suggested affirmation and any suggested tasks to help implement the manifestation..

If you sign-up for a Manifest AI subscription, additional non-personally identifiable data is kept. Your billing information, address, and name are all maintained by the App Store, but these are invisible to me. I will get information about whether or not you have purchased a subscription, the term you signed up for and when it is due to expire. This is data is all anonymous. This anonymous data is also shared with my purchase provider (RevenueCat).
